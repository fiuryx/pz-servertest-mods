title=Has Been Read - Shared Marks (B42)
description=[h1]Has Been Read - Shared Marks (B42)[/h1]
description=Add-on for [b]Has Been Read[/b] (required). Has Been Read lets you mark items ("I already have this book") but the marks are personal. This add-on lets you share them.
description=
description=[h2]New option[/h2]
description=In Options > Mods > Has Been Read there is a new option [b]Shared item marks[/b]. Each player chooses:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Only mine[/b]: your own marks, exactly like the original mod (default).
description=[*][b]Whole server[/b]: your marks plus a list shared by every player who uses this mode.
description=[*][b]My faction[/b]: your marks plus your faction's list (vanilla factions).
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]In the shared modes, marking or unmarking an item (by hand, with "mark all" or with auto-mark) updates that list for everyone sharing it, at once.
description=[*]Your own marks are always kept apart. Switch back to Only mine and you see just yours again.
description=[*]Your existing marks become your own marks the first time you play with this add-on.
description=[*]The lists are saved on the server with the world.
description=[*]Works with every item Has Been Read can mark: skill books, recipe magazines, titled books, fliers, brochures, maps, CDs and VHS tapes.
description=[/list]
description=
description=Needs to be on the server (it keeps the shared lists). In single player it does nothing. Does not modify Has Been Read itself. All credit for Has Been Read goes to its author.
