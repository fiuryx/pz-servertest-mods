description=[h1]Defecation - Clean Soiled Clothes Fix (B42)[/h1]
description=The [b]Defecation[/b] mod lets you clean soiled clothes at a water source with bleach or cleaning liquid. It adds that option inside the water source submenu, looking the submenu up by its name. Since Build 42.22 builds that menu in Java, the lookup can fail and the option never appears, so soiled clothes cannot be cleaned.
description=
description=This patch checks the menu after Defecation has filled it. If the option is missing, it adds it: inside the water source submenu when it can find it, otherwise at the top of the menu.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Same rules as Defecation: 300 ml of bleach or cleaning liquid and 7 L of water within 10 tiles.
description=[*]Uses Defecation's own wash action and texts, so the result is identical (clean, wet clothes with their original name).
description=[*]If Defecation's own option works, this patch does nothing, so it never shows twice.
description=[*]Client side only. Safe to add or remove at any time, also on servers.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires[/b] Defecation.
