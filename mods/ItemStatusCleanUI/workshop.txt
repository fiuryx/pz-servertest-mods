title=Item Status CleanUI (B42)
description=[h1]Item Status CleanUI (B42)[/h1]
description=Shows coloured bars under the item icon in the CleanUI inventory and loot windows: condition of weapons and tools, remaining contents of consumables and freshness of food.
description=
description=[b]Features[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Colour-coded condition bar: green (75-100%), yellow (50-75%), orange (25-50%), red (below 25%).
description=[*]Second bar with the head condition on weapons that have one (axes, spears, hammers...).
description=[*]Works in both CleanUI modes (CleanUI and vanilla layout) and while dragging items.
description=[*]Blue bar with remaining contents of lighters, matches, batteries, tape, thread, liquid containers and magazines (red when almost empty).
description=[*]Food freshness bar on perishable food: time left before it rots, green while fresh, orange when stale, full red when rotten, light blue when frozen. Canned and non-perishable food shows no bar.
description=[*]Closed stacks show the bars of the item in the worst state, so you can spot the nearly empty lighter or the broken axe without expanding the stack.
description=[*]Condition bar on clothing (wear, holes and tears), on by default.
description=[*]Status icons in the corner of the item icon: red drop for blood (clothing, weapons and tools, worn bags), mud stain for dirt (clothing and bags), blue drop for wet items (clothing, books, paper...) and a brown icon for clothing soiled by the Defecation mod (only when that mod is active). The stronger the state, the stronger the colour.
description=[*]Capacity bar on the container buttons on the side of the inventory and loot windows: your inventory, bags, furniture, car trunks and seats. Follows the bar position option.
description=[*][b]Reading status[/b] (replaces Has Been Read): bar on skill books, recipe magazines and clippings, titled books, fliers, brochures, maps, CDs and VHS tapes. Light blue: not read or watched yet. Purple: half read (pages read), or you know some of the recipes. Green: read, watched or listened to, or the skill book has nothing left to teach you. Always what [i]you[/i] did, never shared between players.
description=[*][b]"I have it" mark[/b]: right-click to mark books, magazines, fliers, maps and discs you already have (by type: every "Mechanics Vol.2" shows as marked), or let them be marked when you pick them up. A small bookmark icon shows on marked items. Each player chooses to see only their own marks, the whole server's list or their faction's list. Marks from Has Been Read are imported the first time.
description=[*]Info box when the mouse is over a bar or an icon: the state and its percentage, and for food the days left before it goes stale and rots (off by default, turn it on in the options).
description=[*]Choose where the bars go: along the bottom or top of the icon, or vertical on the left or right side (bottom by default).
description=[*]Hooks into CleanUI's row renderer without replacing it, so it does not conflict with other inventory overlays. The hotbar is left to Clean HotBar.
description=[*]Client-side drawing only: it reads the condition the server already syncs and saves nothing. Multiplayer and dedicated server safe; can be added or removed at any time.
description=[*]English and Spanish texts.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Options[/b] (Options > Mods > Item condition bars): enable/disable, bar position, show on weapons and tools, show on clothing, show weapon head condition, show remaining contents, show food freshness, show bars on closed stacks, hide when in perfect condition, mark bloody, dirty and wet items, mark soiled clothing (Defecation), show info on bars and icons.
description=
description=[b]Requires[/b] CleanUI [B42].
description=
description=[b]Changelog[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]1.3: clothing bars on by default; new "Bar position" option (bottom, top, left or right; bottom by default). Separate option for the weapon and tool condition bar.

description=[*]1.5: better performance with big closed stacks (hundreds of nails or bullets): the worst item is cached for a second and at most 200 items per stack are checked.

description=[*]1.9: reading progress bar on skill books (pages read by you), purple while reading and green when finished, with its own option.
description=[*]1.10: new option "Bar on finished books" (on by default). Turn it off to see the reading bar only while a book is being read, useful with mods like Has Been Read.
