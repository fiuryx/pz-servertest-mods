description=In vanilla Build 42 only livestock and horse trailers can carry animals. This mod gives the vehicles you already have a small animal space in their trunk or truck bed, using the game's own livestock trailer system.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Cars and SUVs[/b]: 60 (birds, small animals, a lamb).
description=[*][b]Pickups and trucks[/b]: 120 (a calf or a sheep, or several small animals).
description=[*][b]Vans, step vans and buses[/b]: 200 (about two sheep).
description=[*][b]Cargo trailers[/b]: 150. Livestock trailers keep their own capacity.
description=[/list]
description=Animal sizes are the vanilla ones (hen 30, lamb 60, piglet 70, sheep 100, calf 120, cow 400), so cows never fit in a car.
description=
description=[list]
description=[*]Works on existing vehicles, vanilla and modded, already parked in your world: nothing to spawn, no save changes.
description=[*]Same vanilla menus: put animals in (from your arms or leading them), check the animals inside, take them out. The trunk door must be open.
description=[*]The animals stay real: hunger, thirst, age, genetics.
description=[*]Only vehicles with a trunk or truck bed get it; burnt and smashed wrecks are skipped.
description=[/list]
description=No new texts (it uses the game's own translations). Multiplayer: applied on both server and clients with the same rules.
