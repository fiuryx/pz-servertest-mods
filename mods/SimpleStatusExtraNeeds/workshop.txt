title=Simple Status - Extra Needs (B42)
description=[h1]Simple Status - Extra Needs (B42)[/h1]
description=Adds extra bars to [b]Simple Status[/b] for needs that other mods add, using Simple Status' official API:
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Defecate[/b] ([b]Defecation[/b]): how much your character needs to go. At about 67 you can use the toilet; near 95 you may soil yourself.
description=[*][b]Bladder[/b] ([b]Lifestyle: Hobbies[/b]): need to urinate, 0-100.
description=[*][b]Hygiene[/b] ([b]Lifestyle: Hobbies[/b]): need to wash, 0-100.
description=[*][b]Alcohol withdrawal[/b] ([b]Evolving Traits World[/b]): withdrawal severity of the Alcoholic trait. It stays at 0 if your character is not an alcoholic.
description=[*][b]Sleep schedule[/b] ([b]Evolving Traits World[/b]): how far you are from your preferred bedtime.
description=[/list]
description=Each bar is only added when its mod is active, so it works with any combination of them. All bars are hidden by default: turn on the ones you want from the Simple Status menu, like any other bar.
description=
description=[h2]Details[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Only reads values those mods already store on the player; nothing is changed.
description=[*]Colour goes from green to red as the need grows. Uses each mod's own moodle icons.
description=[*]Each bar has marks at 25, 50 and 75 and the separator line above it. Simple Status bars that had no separator line (weight, carry capacity, dirtiness, nicotine...) get it too, so the list looks even.
description=[*]Bars are sorted in groups: body (health, endurance, fatigue, pain), needs (hunger, thirst, toilet, hygiene, sleep), mood, illness and addictions, temperature and body state, and weight and nutrition. Bars from other mods go at the end.
description=[*]English and Spanish texts.
description=[*]Client side only. Safe to add or remove at any time, also on servers.
description=[/list]
description=[b]Requires[/b] Simple Status.
