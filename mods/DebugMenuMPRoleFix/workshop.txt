description=[list]
description=[*]Debug Menu gives the player a local role called [i]SP_DebugMenu[/i] with every capability when it thinks the game is singleplayer. On dedicated servers that check also passes, so every time an admin right-clicks the world the role is replaced: [i]SP_DebugMenu[/i] appears over the name and the real role (Admin, with its colour and title) is lost on that game.
description=[*]In multiplayer this patch puts the real role back right after the menu is built, and removes the "override need" note that only makes sense in singleplayer.
description=[*]Singleplayer is not touched. Everything else in Debug Menu works as before.
description=[/list]
