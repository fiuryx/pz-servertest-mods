title=Animal Cages and Carriers (B42)
description=[h1]Animal Cages and Carriers (B42)[/h1]
description=In Build 42 a live animal can only be carried in your arms or left on the ground: no bag, crate or trunk accepts it. This mod adds two one-animal containers so you can move small animals the realistic way.
description=
description=[h2]Items[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Small Animal Cage[/b] (1 kg): chicks, hens, roosters, turkey poults, rats, mice and baby rabbits. Fits in large bags (duffel, hiking bag, ALICE...), trunks and crates. Found in pet shops and farms, or crafted with 5 wire and pliers.
description=[*][b]Pet Carrier[/b] (2 kg): everything the small cage takes, plus rabbits, raccoons, turkeys, lambs and piglets. Too big for bags: carry it in hand or put it in trunks and crates. Found in pet shops.
description=[/list]
description=Large animals (sheep, pigs, cows, deer) stay as in vanilla: livestock trailers only.
description=
description=[h2]How it works[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Right-click an animal on the ground with an empty cage in your inventory: [i]Catch ... with ...[/i]. Your character picks it up and puts it in. This also works for rabbits, which vanilla does not let you pick up.
description=[*]Holding an animal: right-click it in your inventory, [i]Put in ...[/i].
description=[*]Right-click a cage with an animal: [i]Take out ...[/i]. The animal goes to your arms, and from there all vanilla options work (drop it, hutch, trailer...).
description=[*]The cage holds the game's own animal item, so the animal keeps its breed, genetics, age, health and name.
description=[*]Being caged is stressful: the animal gets stress for the time it spent inside (up to a limit).
description=[*]No food or water inside: the hunger and thirst of the time spent caged are applied when it comes out, and an animal left too long comes out dead. Rats, mice, chicks, turkey poults and baby rabbits last 2 days; hens, roosters, turkeys, rabbits and raccoons 3 days; lambs and piglets 4 days. The cage name warns you: "thirsty", then "very weak".
description=[*]Nothing else can be put in a cage, and the animal cannot be dragged out by hand.
description=[/list]
