description=[h1]AutoCook & Manage Containers - Range (B42)[/h1]
description=Patch for [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3388721641]AutoCook[/url] and [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2650547917]Manage Containers[/url]. Both only used the containers next to you. Now they use every container within a radius (8 tiles by default, same floor) and the character walks to each one.
description=
description=[b]AutoCook[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]Ingredients are taken from the fridges, cupboards and other containers within the radius.
description=[*]The character walks to each container to take the ingredient, and at the end walks back to put the unused items where they were.
description=[*]The AutoCook option is enabled when the ingredients are within the radius, not only next to you.
description=[*]When the dish is finished and needs cooking, the character puts it in the nearest free, powered oven within the radius (or a microwave if the dish has no metal) and turns it on. It turns off by itself as soon as the dish is cooked, so it does not burn.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Manage Containers[/b]
description=[list]
description=[*]"Put in assigned containers" uses the configured containers within the radius.
description=[*]The character walks to each container, nearest first, and puts its items in.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[b]Sandbox options[/b]: radius (1 to 20 tiles), on/off for each mod and for cooking in the oven/microwave.
description=
description=Requires AutoCook and Manage Containers. Multiplayer safe: the server starts the oven and turns it off.
