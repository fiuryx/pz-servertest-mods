description=[h1]Container Button Drag (B42)[/h1]
description=Drag a bag straight from its button in the container column of the inventory and loot windows.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=In the vanilla game the buttons on the side of the inventory windows can only be clicked. To move a bag you had to open the container it is in and drag it from the item list. With this mod you can press the bag's button and drag it, just like an item from the list:
description=[list]
description=[*]Drop it on the item list of the other window to take it or put it away.
description=[*]Drop it on a container button of the other window to put it inside that container.
description=[*]Drop a bag you carry on the world to leave it on the ground.
description=[*]A simple click still opens the container as usual. Dropping it back on its own button does nothing.
description=[/list]
description=Works for any container that is an item: backpacks, bags, satchels, key rings, lunch boxes... Furniture, corpses, vehicles and the floor are not affected.
description=
description=[h2]Compatibility[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Uses the game's own drag and drop, so transfers are normal timed actions with the usual weight, capacity and multiplayer checks.
description=[*]Works with the vanilla UI and with [b]CleanUI[/b], Proximity Inventory, Equipment UI and Nested Containers.
description=[*]CleanUI lets you reorder the container column by dragging its buttons. Both work together: while the mouse stays inside the column you are reordering; once it leaves the column the bag is picked up to be moved and the column goes back to how it was.
description=[*]Client side only. Can be added or removed at any time.
description=[/list]
description=Mouse only, player 1 (no split-screen or controller).
description=
description=[b]1.1[/b]: no longer clashes with CleanUI's drag-to-reorder of the container column.
