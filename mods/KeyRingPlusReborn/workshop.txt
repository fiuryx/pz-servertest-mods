title=Key Ring Plus - Reborn (B42)
description=[h1]Key Ring Plus - Reborn (B42)[/h1]
description=An updated remake of [b]Key Ring Plus[/b] by [b]ProduktFrosch[/b], rewritten for Build 42.13 and later. Made in his memory, with the original key ring model and icon.
description=
description=[h2]What it does[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Adds the [b]ProduktFrosch key ring[/b], a memento key ring that holds keys and small survival tools: P38 and regular can openers, bottle openers, corkscrew, Handiknife, multitool, penlight, magnesium firestarter, lighters (refillable, disposable and battery), thread, paperclips, fishing hooks (normal, forged and bone), Authentic Z glow sticks, whistles (normal and bone), pocket knife, tweezers, directional compass and pet tags.
description=[*]Found on shelves, dressers, classrooms and comic stores, or bought with 150 money (Miscellaneous crafting).
description=[*]Balanced like the vanilla key rings: capacity 2, 85 percent weight reduction.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]What changed from the original[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The key ring is a real container in the current item format. The original was created as a plain item and crashed the game ([i]ComboItem cannot be cast to InventoryContainer[/i]) when opening or closing doors.
description=[*]Vanilla items are no longer overridden. The original redefined the can opener, Handiknife, multitool, penlight, magnesium firestarter, lighter and thread with the old format, which broke them after 42.13. This version uses its own accept function instead, so those items stay exactly as in vanilla.
description=[*]World spawns fixed (the original pointed to an item that did not exist).
description=[*]Translations in the B42 JSON format (English and Spanish).
description=[/list]
description=
description=Do not enable it together with the original Key Ring Plus. Works in singleplayer and multiplayer. New mod ID, so old ProduktFrosch key rings from the original mod are not converted.
description=
description=All credit for the idea, the model and the icon goes to ProduktFrosch. Rest in peace.
description=
description=[h2]Changelog[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]Disposable and battery lighters now fit on the key ring (only the refillable lighter was accepted before).
description=[*]Key ring icon and model texture toned down (less saturated, warmer) so they match the vanilla item style.
description=[/list]
