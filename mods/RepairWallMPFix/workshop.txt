description=[h2]What it fixes[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Materials taken from the wrong player.[/b] The original keeps the scanned inventory in one table shared by everyone on the server. If two players open the repair menu at about the same time, the nails, planks or pipes can be removed from the other player. Now every player has their own pending repair, and the server checks the materials again right before taking them.
description=[*][b]Tools had to be in your hand in MP.[/b] The server tried to send inventory items to the client, and items do not travel over the network. Now the server only sends counts, and your hammer or blowtorch is found in your inventory or equipped bags, moved to your main inventory and equipped for you.
description=[*][b]Metal repairs in MP.[/b] The welding mask was taken from that same empty list, so metal repairs could fail. Now the mask is found and worn automatically.
description=[*][b]Repair amount.[/b] It is computed on the server from your Carpentry or Metalworking level, and the new health is sent to every player.
description=[*]The repair menu also shows up when the first object on the square is already at full health.
description=[*]Names in the menu ("Wall / Object", "Door") are now translatable, and the "Repair ..." option gets a hammer or blowtorch icon.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]With Show Wall Health[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*]The "Check ..." option gets a translated name and an info icon.
description=[*]Answers meant for other players no longer add extra options to your menu.
description=[/list]
